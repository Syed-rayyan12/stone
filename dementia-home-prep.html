Dementia-Sensitive Home Preparation & Downsizing

Restructuring living environments to prevent disorientation, eliminate fall hazards, and preserve precious identity without stripping the warmth of home.

Sensory Calming Environment
Environmental Science & Compassion

The Psychology of the Living Space

For someone living with dementia, Alzheimer's, or acute cognitive decline, visual clutter, ambiguous shadows, reflective flooring, and rearranged furniture can cause intense confusion, sundowning, and dangerous falls.

Standard house clearers or commercial contractors often create trauma by rushing through rooms and treating cherished items as junk. At Stonemere, we treat every possession as a chapter of a human life. We work slowly, calmly, and methodically alongside families to de-escalate anxiety while creating an instinctively navigable home.

Direct Consultation: 07891 341414

Key Safety Outcomes

  • 85% Reduction in Fall Triggers: Removing high-contrast rugs, hidden wire coils, and unstable accent furniture.
  • Visual Anchor Stations: Positioning primary eyeglasses, watches, telephone, and photo albums in clear line of sight.
  • Circadian Lighting Adjustments: Eliminating deep evening shadows that trigger twilight disorientation and anxiety.
  • Subtle Appliance Safeguards: Discrete auto-shutoffs and locked medication/chemical security without hospital signage.
The Stonemere Standard

Our 4 Environmental Protocols

1. Sightline & Wayfinding

Creating unobstructed direct pathways from bedroom to bathroom and kitchen, ensuring key destinations are intuitively visible at all hours.

2. Memory Curation & Keepsakes

Constructing personalised memory display cabinets containing high-resonance family photographs, awards, and sensory keepsakes.

3. Gentle Paced Downsizing

When relocating to a retirement suite, we recreate the familiar aesthetic, chair placement, and artwork arrangement to prevent transition trauma.

4. Discrete Hazard Mitigation

Securing gas isolators, hot water thermostatic valves, and trip risks invisibly, preserving the dignified luxury feel of a private residence.

Relocation & Assisted Living

Transitioning to Supported Living Without Distress

Moving from a 4-bedroom family home to a luxury care apartment can feel like an impossible emotional mountain. Stonemere manages the entire physical process from start to finish.

Pre-Move Space Mapping

We measure the new suite and select favorite armchairs, bedside tables, and artworks so the new space instantly feels like home.

White-Glove Move Day

While your loved one enjoys a calm lunch with family, our team completely unpacks, makes beds, and hangs pictures before they arrive.

Original House Resolution

We then manage the remaining property clearance, deep cleaning, and security checks ready for estate appraisal or sale.
