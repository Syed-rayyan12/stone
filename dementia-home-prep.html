WHERE COMPASSION MEETS THE DESIGN

What a Living Space Does to the Mind

For someone living with dementia, Alzheimer's, or significant cognitive decline, clutter, ambiguous shadows, reflective flooring, and furniture that's moved around can trigger real confusion, sundowning, and dangerous falls.

Standard clearance firms often rush through rooms and treat cherished belongings as junk, which is distressing to watch. At Stonemere, every possession is treated as part of someone's story. We move slowly and methodically alongside the family, easing anxiety while building a home that's instinctive to navigate.

Direct Consultation: 07891 341414

Where Safety Shows Up

  • 5% Reduction in Fall Triggers: high-contrast rugs, hidden wire coils, and unstable accent furniture, removed.
  • Visual Anchor Stations: glasses, watch, telephone, and photo albums, kept in clear, consistent line of sight.
  • Circadian Lighting Adjustments: deep evening shadows removed, so twilight disorientation has less to feed on.
  • Subtle Appliance Safeguards: discreet auto-shutoffs and locked medication storage, with no hospital-style signage.
THE STONEMERE METHOD

Four Environmental Protocols

1. Sightline & Wayfinding

Clear, direct pathways from bedroom to bathroom and kitchen, ensuring every key destination stays visible and obvious, day or night.

2. Memory Curation & Keepsakes

Personalised memory displays, built around the family photographs, awards, and keepsakes that mean the most to them.

3. Gentle Paced Downsizing

When it's time to move to a retirement suite, we rebuild the familiar layout down to the chair placement and artwork, to soften the shock of change.

4. Discrete Hazard Mitigation

Gas isolators, thermostatic valves, and trip risks are all secured out of sight, so the home still feels like a private residence, not a facility.

Relocation & Assisted Living

A Move to Supported Living, Without the Distress

Leaving a family home of decades for a care apartment can feel like an impossible emotional climb. Stonemere manages the entire physical move, start to finish, so the family doesn't have to.

Pre-Move Space Mapping

We measure the new suite well ahead of time and choose which armchairs, bedside tables, and artworks travel with them.

White-Glove Move Day

While your relative enjoys a calm lunch out with family, our team unpacks everything, makes the bed, and hangs every picture first.

Original House Resolution

We then handle everything left behind: clearance, deep cleaning, and security checks, ready for valuation or sale.
