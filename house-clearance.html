Dignity First. Clarity Always. Never Guesswork.

Clearing a late parent's home, or an elderly relative's before residential care, is one of the hardest milestones a family faces.

Where standard clearance firms load skips indiscriminately, Stonemere works to strict forensic and probate standards. Every drawer, attic, and cupboard gets checked properly. We track down missing legal paperwork, set aside family heirlooms, catalogue valuable art and jewellery, and hand executors and solicitors a transparent audit trail.

Call Paul Harrison: 07891 341414

The Five-Point Clearance Audit

  • Document Recovery: share certificates, title deeds, wills, military records, and bank books, tracked down.
  • Photographic Inventory: a digital catalogue of every room and significant item, for the family and the executor.
  • Auction Valuation: direct liaison with leading regional and London salerooms for furniture, silver, and clocks.
  • Ethical Charity Donation: verified local charities, with full gift-aid receipts for every item given.
  • Deep Clean & Security: handover ready for estate agent photography, a surveyor visit, or sale.
