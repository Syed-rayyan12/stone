Ethical Estate Resolution

Dignity Over Speed. Transparency Over Guesswork.

Clearing the home of a deceased parent or an elderly relative moving into residential care is one of life's most challenging milestones.

Unlike standard commercial clearance companies that load skips indiscriminately, Stonemere operates under strict forensic and probate standards. Every single drawer, attic, and cupboard is examined. We locate missing legal documents, isolate family heirlooms, catalog valuable art and jewellery, and deliver transparent audit trails for executors and solicitors.

5-Point Probate Clearance Audit

  • Document Recovery: Locating share certificates, title deeds, wills, military records, and bank books.
  • Photographic Inventory: Digital catalog of every room and significant item for family and executor distribution.
  • Auction Valuation: Direct liaison with leading regional and London salerooms for fine furniture, silver, and clocks.
  • Ethical Charity Donation: Partnering with verified local charities, providing full gift aid item receipts.
  • Deep Clean & Security: Handover ready for estate agent photography, surveyor inspection, or sale.
