In-Depth Pillars

Three Disciplines, In Detail

Crisis Coordination & a Safe Discharge Home

DEPLOYED ON-SITE WITHIN 24 HOURS

A sudden illness, a fall, a hospital stay: the home is often nowhere near ready for the return. We're on-site fast to arrange a key box, handle the small but critical adaptations (stair handrails, a repositioned bed, trip hazards cleared), get the heating running again, and stock the essentials in the pantry.

Call 07891 341414

Property Maintenance, Trusted Trades & Security

GUARDING AGAINST ESTATE DECAY

A leaking roof, a damp patch, an overgrown garden: left alone, these knock tens of thousands off an estate's value and unsettle whoever still lives there. Stonemere runs the project end to end, sourcing competitive quotes from vetted, insured trades, supervising the work on-site, and signing off only once it's actually finished properly.

Administrative Organising & Mail Sorting

CUTTING THROUGH THE PAPER BACKLOG

Unopened bills, unpaid council tax, a lost share certificate, a policy nobody remembers taking out: paperwork like this leaves people exposed. We work through it methodically, digitise what matters, cancel what's no longer needed, and hand over clean, indexed binders to the family or the executor.
