No. We're not CQC-regulated, and we don't provide nursing, regulated medical treatment, or hands-on personal care of any kind. What we do instead is coordinate the living environment around someone: managing contractors, untangling administration, and liaising directly with the GPs, solicitors, and social workers already involved in their case.

Day to day, we operate across Greater London, Surrey, Berkshire, Hampshire, and the wider Home Counties. For a full estate clearance, an overseas family repatriation, or a larger ongoing retainer, we regularly travel further afield and arrange shipping internationally.

Nothing gets thrown away without care or consultation. We work at the individual's own pace, keep visual anchor stations for the objects that matter most to them, remove fall hazards early on, and stay closely in step with whoever is already managing their day-to-day care and wellbeing.

Every item is photographed and catalogued before anything is moved or touched. High-value art, jewellery, and silver are passed to RICS or NAVA-accredited appraisers for formal valuation, and executors receive full documented receipts for everything recovered from the property.
