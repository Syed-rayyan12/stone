Meet the Director

Paul Harrison

"When a family crisis unfolds or a parent's health declines, the emotional weight is compounded by hundreds of practical obstacles. Stonemere exists to lift that operational burden completely."

With decades of high-level coordination experience, Paul Harrison leads Stonemere with a blend of meticulous attention to detail and profound human empathy. Having witnessed how standard institutional systems repeatedly fail families through lack of personal continuity, Paul established a boutique service dedicated to total accountability.
